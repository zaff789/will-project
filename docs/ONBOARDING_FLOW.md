# ONBOARDING_FLOW.md — The Will

Locked UX journey from arrival to "ready" (director-approved). Bilingual EN/TH,
calm and reverent, honest early. Full ~9-screen depth; ends offering both doors.

## Visual system
- **Sacred edges = warm-dark atmosphere** (landing, onboarding opening, recipient's
  final moment): deep warm ink dissolving into a single warm-gold light/threshold
  (refs: Clicks "threshold of light" + CURRENT warm rim-light family-office). Thin,
  spacious, elegant display type. Film grain, slow light drift.
- **Working app = light warm paper** (dashboard, creation, management): calm,
  readable, elder-friendly. DESIGN.md: go dark only at threshold moments.

## Spine reference
Primary: ABY Journal onboarding (one calm question per screen). Supporting:
Ahead (commitment gesture), Liven (single-select segment picker), Facebook
Legacy Contact (reassuring death-contingent language).

## The journey
- **0.1 Landing hero** — ethereal warm threshold; "The Will" wordmark + bilingual
  tagline; CTA Begin / Sign in. Permanent prototype banner. [Clicks+CURRENT mood;
  Fabric/Telescope layout]
- **1.1 Create your space** — email (mock) / Continue with Google (stub AuthService);
  not-a-legal-will clarifier visible; demo-account shortcut (Anan / Somchai / Ploy).
  [Base44]
- **2.1 A gentle opening** — full-screen still statement, no form. [ABY welcome]
- **2.2 What should we call you?** — name field. [ABY "What should I call you?"]
- **2.3 What brings you here?** — single-select: a memory · last words · private
  instructions · something I've never said. [ABY "What brings you here?"]
- **2.4 Who is this for?** — Yourself · Your family · Your business → Personal /
  Family / Founder. [Liven "Choose your main goal"]
- **2.5 The honest part** — exact CONTENT.md disclaimer EN+TH, reassuring framing,
  required acknowledge. (Onboarding disclaimer instance per CLAUDE.md §6.) [FB Legacy]
- **2.6 How it keeps things safe** — held privately, opens on your conditions +
  screenshot-honesty line. Trust via candor. [ABY reassurance screen]
- **2.7 A deliberate beginning** — press-and-hold "I'm ready to begin." [Ahead promise]
- **3.1 Your space is ready** — quiet, never celebratory. Two doors: Write your
  first message (→ 6-dimension creation) OR See my space (→ dashboard). [ABY/Liven]

## Build order

handoff (3.1) → wire into existing app shell/dashboard.
